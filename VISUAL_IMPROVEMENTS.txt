VISUAL ELEMENTS:
  ░░ = Dark brown square (playable)
  ○  = White piece
  ●  = Red piece
  ♔  = King piece (crowned)
  ◉  = Valid move (green circle)
  [□]= Last move indicator (orange/yellow)

                    ⌨️ UPDATED CONTROLS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

MOUSE:
  Left Click    → Select piece (your color only)
  Left Click    → Move to green circle (valid move)
  
KEYBOARD:
  R key         → Restart current game
  M key         → Return to main menu
  1-4 keys      → Menu selections
  
VISUAL FEEDBACK:
  Selected piece     → Green circles appear
  Last move          → Orange FROM + Yellow TO
  Your turn          → Turn indicator (colored)
  AI thinking        → Window title shows "AI Thinking..."
