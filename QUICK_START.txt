HOW TO RUN:
-----------
1. Double-click "run_checkers.bat" 
   OR
2. Open terminal and type: python main.py

GAME MODES:
-----------
Press 1 - Human vs Human (2 players on same computer)
Press 2 - Human vs AI (Play against computer)
Press 3 - AI vs AI (Watch 2 AIs play)
Press 4 - Quit

IN-GAME CONTROLS:
-----------------
MOUSE:
  • Click piece to select
  • Click green circle to move

KEYBOARD:
  • R = Restart game
  • M = Back to menu

RULES:
------
• Red moves first (bottom pieces)
• Move diagonally forward
• Jump to capture opponent pieces
• Reach opposite end to become King (♔)
• Kings can move backward too
• Win by capturing all opponent pieces

TIPS:
-----
✓ Green circles show valid moves
✓ Protect your back row
✓ Try to make Kings
✓ AI takes 2-5 seconds to think
✓ Multiple jumps allowed in one turn

TROUBLESHOOTING:
----------------
Problem: Game won't start
Solution: Make sure pygame is installed
         Run: pip install pygame

Problem: AI too slow
Solution: Normal! AI is thinking (2-5 seconds)

Problem: Can't click pieces
Solution: Wait for your turn (check turn indicator)
